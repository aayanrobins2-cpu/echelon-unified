About E-Waste Recycling

Students will create a usable product using e-waste and will bring it to the school if possible.

Rules

  • The team must submit a recording of how the product was created from e-waste.
  • Number of teams per school: 2
  • Number of participants per team: Minimum 2, Maximum 4
  • Grade Level: 6-12
