Plaksha

Plaksha is a technology university for the future and has been set up by a global community of entrepreneurs, business leaders, and academicians to reimagine technology education and research. The vision of the university is to nurture the next generation of fearless leaders who will solve the toughest challenges of our planet.

Vidyashilp

Vidyashilp University is the culmination of an outstanding 4-decade legacy of the Vidyashilp Education Group. The university offers an interdisciplinary learning experience that is intellectually, personally, and socially transformative. With a carefully curated curriculum, the university aims to nurture the next generation of change-makers who are endowed with the knowledge and skills to address the grand challenges of a globalized world.
