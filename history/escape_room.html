About Escape Room

A team of 4 students will be locked in a glass room and will have to fix errors in 5 programs (Java or Python) to unlock a final clue as to where the key is kept. They will use this key to escape from the room.

Rules

  • Number of teams per school: 2(Max)
  • Number of participants: minimum 2, maximum 4
  • Duration: 25 mins
  • Resources: Programs will be run from desktops/laptops in school.
  • Prior Knowledge: Java or Python
  • Grade Level: 9-12
