About Shark Tank

This event is recreating the famous TV show "Shark Tank". But the ideas should be related to the 10th SDG of the UN, i.e., "Reducing Inequalities" . It could be an App or Project plan or even a Business Plan aiming to diversify education towards all groups. The Team should talk about the proposal and submit a word document of their proposal.

Rules

  • Teams must create a product or service pitch.
  • Number of teams per school: Maximum 2
  • Number of participants per team: Minimum 1, Maximum 2
  • Grade Level: 9-12
