Small Steps, Big Impact

Teamwork that takes us further

Echelon is the V-shaped formation birds use when migrating. Birds at the back conserve energy before taking their turn to lead the pack. This teamwork is what we hope students will emulate through Echelon, our technology fest.

The theme for every event this year is “Small Steps, Big Impact”. Interpret it any way you want—be creative. Students in Grades 8–12 come together at Bangalore International School, in collaboration with Vidyashilp University, to turn small ideas into meaningful results.

  • Eight ways to compete

    Escape a locked room, find treasure, design websites and games, build with e-waste, race drones, pitch innovations and create an expert system in SWI-Prolog.

  • Teams from your school

    Like migrating birds sharing the lead, work together, support your teammates and build on one another’s ideas.

  • One day on campus

    Everything happens at Bangalore International School, from 8:15 am to 2:30 pm. Breakfast and lunch are provided.

Your event flow

  1. 01

    Choose your challenge

    Explore eight events on “Small Steps, Big Impact”. Seven start at 9:30 am; Treasure Hunt starts at 10:15 am. Check overlapping times before choosing your events.

  2. 02

    Team up and register

    Form a team of three and register on the official form. Each school can enter up to two teams per event.

  3. 03

    Prepare your materials

    Check your event’s rules and equipment. Save Web Designing files locally and bring e-waste or a drone if required. For Pitch Tank, bring your device on the day or email the presentation before 19 November 2026.

  4. 04

    Check in and compete

    Report by 8:15 am for registration and breakfast. Attend the 9:00 am opening, then head to your event’s room. Competitions finish by 12:15 pm; lunch is at 12:45 pm and prizes at 1:30 pm.

Questions, answered

Taking part

Who can take part?

Students in Grades 8–12.

How do we register?

Through the official registration form linked on this page. It opens in a new tab. This website doesn't collect any details itself.

How big is a team, and how many can a school send?

Every team has three students, and each school can enter up to two teams in each event.

Are AI tools allowed?

AI tools are not permitted in Web Designing or Game Designing. Pitch Tank permits AI during the development phase. Web Designing also prohibits templates and has no internet access.

On the day

When and where is it?

On Saturday, 21 November 2026, at Bangalore International School. Report by 8:15 am. The day ends with prize distribution, from 1:30 to 2:30 pm.

Is food provided?

Yes. Breakfast (8:15 to 9:00 am) and lunch (12:45 to 1:20 pm) are provided.

What should we bring?

Web Designing: save all required files locally before starting; internet will be disabled. Game Designing: bring your laptop or use the Windows PCs provided. E-Waste Reimagined: bring your own e-waste and complete all work during the competition. Drone Racing: bring your drone and batteries; the drone must be at most 30 × 30 cm and under 250 g. Use propeller guards and do not use damaged batteries. Teams may share drones. Pitch Tank: bring your device or email your presentation to mahenthren@bischool.in before 19 November 2026.

Who do we contact with questions?

Email the organising team at malarvizhi@bischool.in.
